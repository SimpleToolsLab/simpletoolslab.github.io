About SimpleToolsLab

Learn about our free writing tools, how they work and their limitations.

What we build

SimpleToolsLab provides lightweight, browser-based writing utilities. The Word Counter helps check word and sentence totals and extract text from common document formats. The Character Counter tracks length against a chosen limit, and the Case Converter changes letter capitalization.

We aim to make the tools usable without account creation and to explain what each count means, including where results may differ from word processors and online forms.

Limitations

Counts and conversions are automated estimates. The Word Counter does not recognize scanned images inside PDFs, and formatting conversions may need manual review. Please follow your institution's or publisher's rules when exact counting conventions matter.
