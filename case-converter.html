How each conversion works

UPPERCASE and lowercase

Uppercase turns letters into capitals, while lowercase turns them into small letters. Numbers and most punctuation stay the same.

Title Case

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of each word. This is a simple formatting transformation, not a publisher-specific title capitalization style. For example, it may capitalize short words such as “and” and “of.”

Sentence case

Sentence case lowercases the text and capitalizes the first letter of the text and letters after sentence-ending punctuation. It is a basic helper and may not preserve proper nouns, acronyms or names, so always review the result before using it.
