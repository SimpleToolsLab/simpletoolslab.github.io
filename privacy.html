Privacy Policy

How SimpleToolsLab handles text, document extraction, analytics and advertising services.

Text and documents

The Word Counter, Character Counter and Case Converter process entered text in your browser. TXT, DOCX and text-based PDF extraction on the Word Counter page also runs in the browser. We do not provide a SimpleToolsLab server endpoint for uploading the text you enter into these tools.

Document extraction uses third-party JavaScript libraries loaded by the browser. Avoid entering highly sensitive information unless you are comfortable with the website and the services it loads.

Analytics and advertising

Our pages use Google Analytics to understand website traffic. The Word Counter page also includes Google AdSense and Infolinks scripts. These third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies and receive information such as your IP address, device and browser details under their own privacy policies. Their use of data is governed by their policies, not by the local text-processing functions.

For more information, see Google's privacy policy and Infolinks' privacy policy.

Choices and contact

You can clear entered text using the tool's Clear button or by closing the page. Clearing the editor does not delete information independently collected by third-party services. Browser settings can help you manage cookies and tracking preferences.

For questions about this website, use the contact details on our contact page.
